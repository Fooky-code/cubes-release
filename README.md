CUBES — Ice Business POS
Point-of-sale app for an ice business. Android tablet, 7" and up.

**Download**
[Download CUBES 1.0.0 — APK](https://github.com/Fooky-code/cubes-release/releases/download/v1.0.0/CUBES-1.0.0.apk)

**Install**
Tap the download link above, on the tablet.
Download the .apk file. If Chrome asks for permission to download, allow it.
Open the downloaded file — it appears in your notifications or in Downloads.
Android will say it can't install apps from this source. Tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then press back.
Tap Install.
If Play Protect says the app is unsafe, tap More details → Install anyway. That warning appears because the app doesn't come from the Play Store — not because anything is wrong with it.
Open CUBES from the home screen.

**First time**
The app starts empty.

Settings — set your walk-in rate per kilo.
Customers — add your regular customers and their own rates.
Start taking orders.

**Your records**
Everything is stored on the tablet. Nothing is sent anywhere.

**Back up regularly.**
Settings — Export backup saves a file. Keep it somewhere off the tablet — a Google Drive folder, or email it to yourself.

If the tablet is lost, stolen or breaks, that file is how you get your records back. There is no other copy.

**Updating to a new version**
Install the new APK over the old one, the same way you installed the first one. Your records are kept.

Never uninstall the app. Uninstalling deletes every order, payment, customer and expense on the tablet.

**Printing**
Receipts, order slips and reports print through Android's print dialog — Save as PDF or print to a supported printer.

A thermal receipt printer is supported on request.
